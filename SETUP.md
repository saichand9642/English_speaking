# Speak — your English coach. Setup in 15 minutes.

You do this once. After that you just tap the icon on your iPhone.

---

## Step 1 — Get your free Google AI key (2 min)

1. Go to **https://aistudio.google.com/apikey**
2. Sign in with any Google account
3. Tap **Create API key** → **Create API key in new project**
4. Copy the key (starts with `AIza…`) and keep it somewhere for a moment

No credit card. No payment. If Google ever asks you to enable billing, you are on the wrong page — go back to the link above.

---

## Step 2 — Put the app online (10 min)

The microphone only works on a real `https://` address, so the file has to be hosted. GitHub Pages does this free, forever.

1. Create a free account at **https://github.com**
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**
   - Name: `speak`
   - Choose **Public**
   - Tick **Add a README file**
   - Click **Create repository**
3. On the repository page click **Add file** → **Upload files**
4. Drag in `index.html`, then click **Commit changes**
5. Go to the **Settings** tab → **Pages** (left sidebar)
6. Under *Branch*, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, click **Save**
7. Wait 1–2 minutes, then refresh. GitHub shows your link:

   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/speak/`

That link is your app.

> A public repo means the *code* is public. Your API key is **not** in the code — it is typed in on your phone and stored only there.

---

## Step 3 — Install it on your iPhone (1 min)

1. Open your link in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome, for the home-screen icon to work properly)
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow, at the bottom)
3. Scroll down → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**
4. Open it from your home screen. It now runs full-screen like a normal app.

---

## Step 4 — First use

1. Paste your API key, tap **Save & start**
2. Tap the gear icon and type your first language (e.g. *Telugu*) — this makes the pronunciation tips much more useful
3. Tap the microphone, say a sentence out loud, tap it again to stop
4. Allow the microphone when Safari asks — this appears only the first time

---

## How to actually use it every day

Fifteen minutes, and it works best in this order:

1. **Read the prompt out loud and answer it in 2–3 sentences.** Don't plan the sentence first — speak it the way you would in a real conversation. Corrections only help if the mistake was a real one.
2. **Read the "What to fix" explanations, then say the corrected sentence out loud.** Saying it is what moves it into your speech. Reading it only moves it into your memory.
3. **Tap "Hear it correctly", then repeat it, matching the rhythm** — not just the sounds. English rhythm carries more meaning than most learners expect.
4. **Once a week, open "Mistakes you keep making"** and try to speak sentences that use those exact structures on purpose.

The recurring mistakes list is the real product. Everyone makes 5–10 errors on a loop; fixing those 5–10 is most of the improvement available to you.

## If something goes wrong

**"I couldn't get microphone access"** — Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow. Then close and reopen the app.

**"That API key was rejected"** — Tap the gear icon and paste it again, with no spaces before or after.

**"You've hit today's free limit"** — The free tier resets. Wait a few minutes, or come back tomorrow. Normal daily practice will not get near the limit.

**Nothing happens when you tap record** — You opened the file directly instead of the `https://` link. The microphone needs the hosted address.

**Corrections seem wrong or too harsh** — Tell me and I will adjust the coaching instructions inside the app.
